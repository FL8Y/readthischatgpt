# 1000/1000 Architecture Quality Gate

**Target rating: 1000/1000.** The architect's commitment is to work toward 10/10 in every category and award 1000/1000 only after the documented criteria pass. This is a target, not a falsely pre-awarded result.

The app remains in planning-only mode. No implementation is authorized.

The final 1000/1000 trace must include evidence for product scope, requirements traceability, learning behavior, architecture decisions, data contracts, AI provenance, UX/accessibility, test design, research integrity, and documentation/agent governance. Each category must independently pass its acceptance gate; an average cannot hide a failing category.

Personal-use v1 assumption: Android-first, local-first, no mandatory backend or sync. Keep baseline credential hygiene, safe imports, data recovery, and nursing-content safeguards without overbuilding enterprise security.

A score may be recorded as 1000/1000 only when all category gates pass, repository changes are read back and verified, known material blockers are resolved or shown not to block the planning stage, and no app implementation is claimed or performed. Record iteration date, commit SHA, checks, findings closed/open, and reviewer participation honestly.
