## Core concept
Primary Health Care is a whole-of-society approach combining integrated health services, multisectoral action, and empowered communities. Classic Alma-Ata teaching lists eight essential elements; distinguish this historical list from current PHC/UHC frameworks.

## High-yield points
- Classic elements: health education; food/nutrition; safe water/sanitation; maternal-child health/family planning; immunization; endemic disease control; common disease/injury treatment; essential drugs.
- Primary care is a service-delivery component; PHC is broader.
- RA 11223 (Universal Health Care Act) is the key Philippine system-reform framework.

## Community nursing workflow
1. **Assess:** define client/population, baseline status, risks, protective factors, barriers, and data sources.
2. **Prioritize:** address life-threatening and safety concerns first; then consider burden, inequity, preventability, feasibility, and community priorities. Maslow can help organize needs but must not delay urgent intervention.
3. **Plan/implement:** co-design evidence-based actions, assign roles, communicate clearly, document, and use appropriate referral pathways.
4. **Evaluate:** compare outcomes to baseline and targets; revise the plan when evidence or community feedback indicates a gap.
