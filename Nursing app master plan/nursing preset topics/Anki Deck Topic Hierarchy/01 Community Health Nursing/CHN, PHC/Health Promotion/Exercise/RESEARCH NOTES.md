## Core concept
PHC combines integrated health services, multisectoral action and empowered communities. Classic Alma-Ata teaching lists eight essential elements; distinguish this historical list from current PHC/UHC policy.

## High-yield points
- Classic elements: health education; food/nutrition; water/sanitation; maternal-child health/family planning; immunization; endemic disease control; common disease/injury care; essential drugs.
- Primary care is a service-delivery component; PHC is broader.
- RA 11223 is the Philippine Universal Health Care Act.

## Nursing application
1. Assess population/client, baseline status, risks, protective factors, barriers and data sources.
2. Prioritize immediate safety threats, then burden, inequity, preventability, feasibility and community priorities.
3. Co-design evidence-based actions, assign roles, document, educate and refer appropriately.
4. Evaluate measurable outcomes against baseline and revise using data/community feedback.

## Safety and PNLE distinctions
Protect autonomy, confidentiality, consent, equity and professional scope. Separate Philippine law/policy from international recommendations and older textbook summaries. Verify exact schedules, thresholds, regimens and legal coverage before treating them as definitive.
