# Emergency Services

**Topic path:** `Elements of Primary Health Care/Treatment of Communicable and Noncommunicable Diseases and Emergency Services/Emergency Services`  
**Status:** Initial research note; topic-specific verification required.  
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-17

## Core concept
Cover community prevention, screening, initial support within scope, continuity and referral. Follow current case definitions/reporting and infection-prevention rules; screening alone does not establish diagnosis.

## High-yield points
- For emergencies, assess ABCs rapidly, provide basic support within training and activate referral/EMS.
- Use standard and transmission-based precautions as indicated.
- Verify current DOH disease-specific reporting, treatment and contact-management guidance.

## Nursing application
1. Assess the client/population, baseline status, risks, protective factors, barriers and data sources.
2. Prioritize immediate safety threats, then burden, inequity, preventability, feasibility and community priorities.
3. Co-design evidence-based actions, educate, document and refer appropriately.
4. Evaluate measurable outcomes against baseline and revise using evidence and community feedback.

## Safety and PNLE distinctions
Protect autonomy, consent, confidentiality, equity and professional scope. Separate Philippine requirements from international recommendations and older textbook summaries. Verify exact schedules, thresholds, laws and program definitions before treating them as definitive.
