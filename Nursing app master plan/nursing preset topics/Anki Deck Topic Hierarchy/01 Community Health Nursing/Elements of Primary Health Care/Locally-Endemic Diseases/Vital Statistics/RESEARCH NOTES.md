## Core concept
Define numerator, denominator, multiplier, period and population at risk. Distinguish counts, rates, ratios and proportions; use current DOH FHSIS definitions and validated population denominators.

## High-yield points
- Incidence: new cases in a defined period; prevalence: all existing cases at a point or over a period.
- State the denominator and reference period.
- Check completeness, timeliness, case definitions and population denominator before comparison.

## Nursing application
1. Assess the client/population, baseline status, risks, protective factors, barriers and data sources.
2. Prioritize immediate safety threats, then burden, inequity, preventability, feasibility and community priorities.
3. Co-design evidence-based actions, educate, document and refer appropriately.
4. Evaluate measurable outcomes against baseline and revise based on data and community feedback.

## Safety and PNLE distinctions
Protect autonomy, consent, confidentiality, equity and professional scope. Separate Philippine requirements from international recommendations and older textbook summaries. Verify exact thresholds, schedules, laws and program definitions before treating them as definitive.
