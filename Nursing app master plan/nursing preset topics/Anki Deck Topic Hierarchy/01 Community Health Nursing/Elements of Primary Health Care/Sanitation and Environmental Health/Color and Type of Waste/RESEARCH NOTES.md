## Core concept
Cover water safety, waste/excreta management, food hygiene and vector control. Verify PD 856, RA 9275, RA 9003, current IRRs and local rules.

## High-yield points
- Prevent contamination from source through household storage.
- Follow applicable waste segregation policy; color alone does not establish compliance.
- Escalate suspected outbreaks and hazardous exposures.

## Nursing application

2. Prioritize immediate safety threats, then burden, inequity, preventability, feasibility and community priorities.
3. Co-design evidence-based actions, educate, document and refer appropriately.
4. Evaluate measurable outcomes against baseline and revise based on evidence/community feedback.
