## Core concept
Follow the current Philippine NIP Manual of Procedures and DOH issuances for schedule, eligibility, dose/route, cold chain, catch-up and AEFI reporting; older EPI tables may be outdated.

## High-yield points
- Verify current DOH schedules before quoting exact ages/doses.
- Quarantine stock after unresolved temperature excursions pending authorized assessment.
- Distinguish true contraindications from precautions and minor illness.

## Nursing application
1. Assess the client/population, baseline, risks, protective factors, barriers and data quality.
2. Prioritize immediate safety threats, then burden, inequity, preventability, feasibility and community priorities.
3. Co-design evidence-based interventions, clarify roles, educate, document and refer appropriately.
4. Evaluate measurable outcomes against baseline and revise using data and community feedback.

## Safety and PNLE distinctions
Protect consent, autonomy, confidentiality, equity and professional scope. Separate Philippine law/policy from international recommendations and older textbook summaries. Verify exact schedules, thresholds, regimens and legal coverage before treating them as definitive.
