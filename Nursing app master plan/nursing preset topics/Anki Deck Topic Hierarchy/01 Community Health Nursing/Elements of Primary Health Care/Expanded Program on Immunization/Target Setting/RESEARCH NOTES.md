# Target Setting

**Topic path:** `Elements of Primary Health Care/Expanded Program on Immunization/Target Setting`  
**Status:** Initial research note; verify topic-specific claims before completion.  
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-17

## Core concept
Cover maternal/newborn continuity, birth preparedness, antenatal/postnatal care, family planning, screening, danger signs and referral using current DOH protocols.

## High-yield points
- Urgently refer maternal/newborn danger signs.
- Respect informed choice, privacy and safeguarding duties.
- Newborn screening is not a diagnosis; ensure follow-up of positive results.

## Nursing application
1. Assess baseline status, risks, protective factors, barriers and data sources.
2. Prioritize urgent safety threats, then burden, inequity, preventability and feasibility.
3. Co-design evidence-based actions, educate, document and refer appropriately.
4. Evaluate outcomes against baseline and revise the plan.

## Safety and PNLE distinctions
Protect consent, autonomy, confidentiality, equity and professional scope. Separate Philippine law/policy from international recommendations and older textbook summaries. Verify exact schedules, thresholds, regimens and legal coverage before treating them as definitive.
