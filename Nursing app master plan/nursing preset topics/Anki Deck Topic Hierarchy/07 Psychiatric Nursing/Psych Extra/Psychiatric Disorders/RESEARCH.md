# Psychiatric Disorders — Scope and Differential Assessment

## Purpose
This broad folder is a scope index, not one diagnosis. Specific disorders require their own focused research files and diagnostic assessment.

## Core assessment
Assess medical stability, mental status, onset/course/duration, functional impairment, substances and medication effects, medical/neurologic causes, trauma, culture/language, and risk to self or others. Use collateral history with appropriate consent and do not diagnose from one symptom or screening score alone.

## Nursing priorities
Immediate physiologic instability, acute confusion, overdose/withdrawal, suicidal intent, severe agitation, and inability to maintain safety require prompt escalation. Use therapeutic communication, non-stigmatizing language, and least-restrictive safe care. Document objective observations and response to interventions.

## Philippine context
RA 11036 establishes a rights-based mental health framework. Verify legal and consent questions against the statute, implementing rules, facility policy, and professional standards.
