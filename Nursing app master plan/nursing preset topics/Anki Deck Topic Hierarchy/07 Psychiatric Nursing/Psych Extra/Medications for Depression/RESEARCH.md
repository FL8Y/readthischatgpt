# Medications for Depression — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
SSRIs/SNRIs can cause GI effects, sexual dysfunction, discontinuation symptoms, bleeding/interactions, and serotonin syndrome. TCAs have anticholinergic effects, orthostasis, cardiac toxicity and potentially fatal overdose. MAOIs have serious drug/food interactions; bupropion lowers seizure threshold.

## Nursing priorities
Screen for bipolar history. Monitor early clinical change, suicidal thinking, activation/mania, adherence, and adverse effects. Do not stop abruptly or combine serotonergic agents without prescriber/pharmacist review; verify current product labeling and individual contraindications.

Use the nursing process and prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Apply Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocols.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
