# Mania (Legacy Folder Placement) — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
Mania is a defining feature of bipolar I disorder, not a subtype of major depressive disorder. This file documents legacy placement without changing repository structure.

## Nursing priorities
Assess elevated/irritable mood, increased energy, decreased need for sleep, pressured speech, grandiosity, racing thoughts, risky activity, psychosis, impairment, and medical/substance causes. Prioritize safety, hydration/nutrition, and rest.

Apply the nursing process; prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Use Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocol.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
