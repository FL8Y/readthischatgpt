# Developmental Disorders — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
Neurodevelopmental disorders begin during development and may affect communication, attention, learning, behavior, or adaptive functioning; examples include autism and ADHD.

## Nursing priorities
Use developmental history, caregiver/school input when appropriate, functional assessment, hearing/vision and medical review. Identify strengths, sensory needs, communication preferences, and co-occurring conditions; avoid assuming behaviors are willful.

Apply the nursing process; prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Use Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocol.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
