# Downers, Uppers, and Hallucinogens — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
Depressants may cause CNS/respiratory depression; stimulants may cause sympathetic overactivity; hallucinogens may cause perceptual changes and impaired judgment. Effects vary by substance and co-ingestion.

## Nursing priorities
Assess ABCs, consciousness, temperature, vital signs, glucose when indicated, trauma, and co-ingestants. Escalate respiratory depression, chest pain, seizures, hyperthermia, severe confusion, or unconsciousness.

Apply the nursing process; prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Use Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocol.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
