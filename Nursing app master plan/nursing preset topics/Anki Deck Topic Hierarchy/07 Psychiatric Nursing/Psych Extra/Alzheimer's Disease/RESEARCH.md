# Alzheimer's Disease — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
Progressive neurodegenerative disease and common cause of dementia; early symptoms often affect recent memory, with later effects on language, judgment, and function.

## Nursing priorities
Assess baseline change, function, medication burden, mood, sleep, sensory impairment, safety, caregiver strain, wandering, driving, cooking, and medication management. Sudden change or fluctuating attention suggests delirium and needs medical evaluation.

Apply the nursing process; prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Use Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocol.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
