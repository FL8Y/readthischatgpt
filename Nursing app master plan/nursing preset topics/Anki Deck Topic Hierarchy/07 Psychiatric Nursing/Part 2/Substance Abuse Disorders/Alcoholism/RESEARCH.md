# Alcohol Use Disorder — PNLE Research

## Clinical concepts
Alcohol use disorder is a problematic pattern of alcohol use causing clinically significant impairment or distress. Intoxication and withdrawal have different presentations and risks.

## Assessment
Assess last drink, amount/pattern, previous withdrawal seizures or delirium tremens, vital signs, tremor, sweating, nausea, confusion, nutrition, liver disease, trauma, and co-ingestants. Withdrawal can progress to seizures and delirium tremens with confusion and autonomic instability.

## Nursing priorities
Severe withdrawal requires urgent monitored treatment under local protocol. Thiamine is important when deficiency/Wernicke encephalopathy is a risk; do not delay emergency glucose treatment for hypoglycemia. Alcohol combined with opioids, benzodiazepines, or other sedatives can cause dangerous CNS/respiratory depression. Use nonjudgmental communication and arrange follow-up treatment.

## Philippine context
Apply RA 11036 rights and confidentiality requirements, local emergency pathways, facility policy, and current scope-of-practice rules.
