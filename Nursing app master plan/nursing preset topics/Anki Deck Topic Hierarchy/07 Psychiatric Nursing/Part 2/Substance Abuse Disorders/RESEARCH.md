# Substance Use Disorders — Core Research

## Core concepts
Substance use disorders involve a problematic pattern of substance use with impaired control, social impairment, risky use, and/or pharmacologic features. Distinguish the disorder from intoxication, withdrawal, tolerance, and physical dependence. Use person-first, non-stigmatizing language.

## Assessment
Ask about substance, amount, route, frequency, last use, co-ingestants, overdose and withdrawal history, prescribed drugs, pregnancy where relevant, mental/physical comorbidity, and patient goals. Assess ABCs, consciousness, vital signs, temperature, trauma, and glucose when indicated. Screen for suicide risk and immediate safety concerns.

## Priority nursing care
Alcohol and sedative withdrawal can be life-threatening; do not abruptly stop chronic use without assessing withdrawal risk. Opioid toxicity can cause respiratory depression and requires emergency response; administer naloxone according to protocol if available and monitor for recurrence. Stimulant toxicity may cause hyperthermia, chest pain, seizures, and severe agitation. Use harm reduction, motivational interviewing, and linkage to evidence-based treatment.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (2018) establishes a rights-based framework for mental health services. Follow local laws, confidentiality requirements, scope of practice, emergency protocols, and facility policy.
