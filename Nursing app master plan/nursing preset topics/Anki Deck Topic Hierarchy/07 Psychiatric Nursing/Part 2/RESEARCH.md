# Part 2 — Disorders and Substances Index — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
This part covers anxiety, OCD, phobic disorders, somatic symptom illness, eating and personality disorders, substance-related conditions, and trauma-related disorders.

## Nursing priorities
Differentiate fear/worry, obsessions/compulsions, trauma-linked symptoms, eating-related medical instability, and intoxication/withdrawal. Prioritize immediate medical threats, self-harm risk, and dangerous withdrawal; use non-stigmatizing language.

Use the nursing process and prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Apply Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocols.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
