# Anxiety-Related Disorders — PNLE-Focused Research

## Core concepts
Anxiety disorders involve excessive fear or anxiety and related behavioral disturbance that causes distress or impairment; the specific pattern and duration distinguish diagnoses.

## Assessment and clinical reasoning
Assessment differentiates generalized anxiety, panic attacks/disorder, social anxiety, specific phobia, OCD-related conditions, trauma-related disorders, and anxiety due to substances or medical illness. Ask about avoidance, function, sleep, comorbidity, substance use, and safety.

## Nursing priorities, safety, and PNLE distinctions
CBT-based treatment is evidence-supported for several anxiety disorders; medication decisions require individual assessment and monitoring. Benzodiazepines can cause sedation, falls, tolerance, dependence, and dangerous respiratory depression with opioids or alcohol; do not combine without explicit clinician direction.

Apply the nursing process and prioritize immediate threats to life and safety over routine teaching. Use ABCs when physiologic instability is present and Maslow as a guide rather than a rigid rule. Individualize care, preserve dignity and rights, document observable findings, and escalate according to local clinical protocols.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) establishes a rights-based national mental health framework. Apply the law and its implementing rules to rights, informed consent, confidentiality, access, and least-restrictive care; consult the actual statutory text and facility policy for case-specific requirements. International guidance does not automatically override Philippine law or local scope-of-practice rules.
