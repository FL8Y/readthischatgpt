# Somatic Symptom Illness — PNLE-Focused Research

## Core concepts
Somatic symptom disorder involves distressing physical symptoms plus excessive thoughts, feelings, or behaviors related to them; genuine medical disease can coexist.

## Assessment and clinical reasoning
Validate suffering, assess symptoms and red flags, review prior evaluation, coordinate consistent care, and assess anxiety/depression, trauma, and functional impact.

## Nursing priorities and safety
Do not imply symptoms are fabricated or dismiss new symptoms. A psychiatric formulation does not rule out medical illness; provide safety-net advice.

Use the nursing process and prioritize immediate threats to life and safety. Apply ABCs when physiologic instability is present; use Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, confidentiality within legal/safety limits, and the least restrictive safe care. Individualize assessment and escalate according to local protocols.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) establishes a rights-based national mental health framework. Apply the statute and its implementing rules to rights, informed consent, confidentiality, and access; verify case-specific legal requirements and local scope-of-practice rules directly.
