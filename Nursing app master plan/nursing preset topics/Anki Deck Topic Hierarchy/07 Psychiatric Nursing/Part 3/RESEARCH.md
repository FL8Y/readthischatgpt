# Part 3 — Mood, Psychosis, and Cognition Index — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
This part includes bipolar disorder, delirium versus dementia, major depressive disorder, and schizophrenia.

## Nursing priorities
Screen for suicide risk in mood disorders, medical causes of acute confusion, psychosis-related safety concerns, medication adverse effects, and changes from baseline. Distinguish delirium's acute/fluctuating attention change from chronic cognitive decline.

Use the nursing process and prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Apply Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocols.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
