# Bipolar Disorder — PNLE Research Notes

## Core concepts
Bipolar disorders involve episodes of mania/hypomania and often depression. Mania causes marked mood/energy change and may involve severe impairment, psychosis, or hospitalization.

## Nursing priorities
Assess sleep need, energy, speech, grandiosity, impulsivity, psychosis, self-neglect, suicide risk, substances, and medical mimics. Lithium requires serum/renal/thyroid monitoring; valproate carries major fetal risks. During mania reduce stimulation and support rest, hydration, nutrition, and safety.

Apply the nursing process; prioritize ABCs and immediate threats to life/safety. Use Maslow as a guide, not a rigid algorithm. Preserve dignity, autonomy, and confidentiality within legal/safety limits; escalate according to local protocol.

## Philippine context
Republic Act No. 11036 (Philippine Mental Health Act, 2018) provides a rights-based framework for mental health services. Verify legal conclusions against the statute, implementing rules, and facility policy.
