## Cross-cutting clinical-priority links
- Suicide/self-harm: assess directly, ensure immediate safety, escalate urgent risk, and follow local protocols; no single screening score replaces clinical assessment.
- Agitation/violence: prioritize safety, medical causes, calm communication, personal space, de-escalation, least-restrictive intervention, and monitoring.
- Medication safety: check contraindications, interactions, adverse effects, pregnancy/lactation and age/organ-function considerations; follow current local formulary and prescriber orders.
- Philippine context: [[RESEARCH LOG]] source register includes RA 11036; verify statutory details against official legal text before topic-specific legal conclusions.
