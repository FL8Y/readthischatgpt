# Sampling Definitions — Nursing Research

- **Target population:** group to which researchers intend to generalize.
- **Accessible/source population:** eligible group available to the study.
- **Sample:** units/participants actually studied.
- **Sampling frame:** operational list or mechanism used to select units.
- **Sampling unit:** unit selected at a sampling stage (person, household, facility, etc.).
- **Inclusion criteria:** characteristics required for participation.
- **Exclusion criteria:** characteristics that prevent inclusion, ideally justified by safety or scientific relevance.
- **Sampling error:** chance difference between a sample estimate and population parameter.
- **Selection bias:** systematic difference caused by how participants enter or remain in the study.
- **Nonresponse bias:** bias when responders differ meaningfully from nonresponders.
- **Representativeness:** degree to which the sample reflects the target population for relevant characteristics; it is not guaranteed by a large n.
- **Power:** probability of detecting a specified effect under assumed conditions; not synonymous with representativeness.

## Nursing application
A study of nurses recruited from one volunteer online group may not represent all Philippine nurses. State recruitment methods, setting, response rate, participant characteristics, and limits on generalization.
