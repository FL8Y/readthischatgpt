## PNLE guardrails
- Match design and analysis to the research question and measurement level.
- Distinguish association from causation, random sampling from random assignment, and statistical from clinical significance.
- Check validity, reliability, sampling bias, confounding, ethics review, informed consent, and privacy protections.
- Use current Philippine research-ethics and privacy requirements where applicable.
