# Research Ethics — Nursing Research

## Ethical foundations
Research ethics protects participants' rights, welfare, dignity, privacy, and fair treatment while supporting trustworthy science. Key requirements include social/scientific value, sound design, fair selection, favorable risk-benefit balance, independent review, informed consent when required, and respect for participants throughout the study.

## Research ethics committee (REC)
Submit the protocol and relevant materials for review before recruitment/data collection when required. REC review considers risk, consent, recruitment, privacy, vulnerable groups, compensation, conflicts, and safety monitoring. Approval is not a substitute for researcher responsibility or ongoing reporting.

## Consent and data protection
Explain purpose, procedures, risks, benefits, alternatives where applicable, confidentiality limits, voluntariness, and withdrawal. Use language participants understand. Collect only necessary data, limit access, secure storage, define retention/disposal, and follow breach procedures. De-identification reduces but may not eliminate re-identification risk.

## Research misconduct
Fabrication invents data; falsification manipulates materials/processes/results; plagiarism presents others' work as one's own. Also avoid selective outcome reporting, undisclosed conflicts, duplicate publication, and improper authorship. Preserve audit trails and report concerns through proper channels.

## Philippine context
Follow the current PHREB National Ethical Guidelines, institutional REC requirements, Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), and applicable DOH/PRC policies. Requirements can differ by study type and institution; verify the currently effective guidance.
