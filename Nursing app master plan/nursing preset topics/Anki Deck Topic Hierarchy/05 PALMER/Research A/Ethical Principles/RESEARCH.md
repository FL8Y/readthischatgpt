# Ethical Principles in Nursing Research — PNLE Research

## Core principles
Human-subject research requires respect for persons/autonomy, beneficence, nonmaleficence, and justice. Researchers must minimize avoidable risk, select participants fairly, protect privacy, obtain valid informed consent when required, and ensure independent ethics review appropriate to the study.

## Consent and vulnerable populations
Explain purpose, procedures, foreseeable risks/benefits, alternatives where relevant, confidentiality limits, voluntariness, and withdrawal rights in understandable language. Additional safeguards may be needed for children, persons with impaired decision-making capacity, dependent relationships, or other vulnerability. Do not assume a signature alone proves understanding or voluntariness.

## Research integrity
Do not fabricate, falsify, plagiarize, manipulate analyses, suppress material adverse findings, or misrepresent authorship. Maintain secure records, disclose conflicts of interest, follow approved protocols, and report unanticipated problems according to ethics and institutional requirements.

## Philippine context
Human health research in the Philippines should follow applicable national ethical guidelines, institutional research ethics committee requirements, privacy law, and relevant DOH/PRC rules. Check the current edition of the Philippine Health Research Ethics Board (PHREB) National Ethical Guidelines and the requirements of the responsible REC. International guidance is not a substitute for local review.

## PNLE high-yield
- Social value does not justify disproportionate or unconsented risk.
- Participation must be voluntary and withdrawal should not trigger improper penalty.
- Ethics review and informed consent are distinct protections; one does not replace the other.
- Privacy safeguards should be planned before collecting identifiable data.
