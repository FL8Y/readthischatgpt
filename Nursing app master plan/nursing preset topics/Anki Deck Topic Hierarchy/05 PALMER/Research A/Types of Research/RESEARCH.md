# Types of Research — Nursing Research

## Classifying research
Research can be classified by purpose, design, approach, setting, time frame, and degree of control. These classifications overlap; one study may be applied, quantitative, quasi-experimental, and prospective at once.

## By purpose
- **Basic/fundamental:** develops knowledge or theory without immediate application as its primary goal.
- **Applied:** addresses a practical problem, such as improving adherence or preventing falls.
- **Evaluation/quality improvement:** examines services or changes against defined aims; whether an activity is research, QI, or both depends on intent, methods, and institutional rules—seek REC/organizational determination rather than self-exempting.
- **Action/participatory research:** involves stakeholders in cycles of inquiry and change.

## By design/approach
Descriptive, correlational, observational analytic, experimental, quasi-experimental, qualitative, and mixed methods are common categories. The research question should determine the design.

## Evidence and ethics
Classifications do not establish quality. Appraise sampling, measurement, bias, analysis, transparency, and applicability. Apply privacy and research-ethics requirements; local REC rules determine whether review is needed.
