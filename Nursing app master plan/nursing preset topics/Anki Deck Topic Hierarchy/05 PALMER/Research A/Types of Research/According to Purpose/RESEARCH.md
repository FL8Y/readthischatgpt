# Types of Research According to Purpose — Nursing Research

## Common categories
- **Basic/pure research:** expands knowledge or tests theory without immediate practical implementation as its main aim.
- **Applied research:** investigates a practical problem and seeks usable findings.
- **Descriptive research:** characterizes a phenomenon, population, or distribution.
- **Explanatory/analytic research:** examines why or how variables relate, while causal strength depends on design.
- **Exploratory research:** investigates a topic with limited prior evidence and develops questions or hypotheses.
- **Evaluative research:** assesses program/process/outcome against criteria.
- **Action/participatory research:** combines inquiry and iterative change with stakeholder involvement.

Categories can overlap and are not mutually exclusive. “Applied” describes purpose, not a specific design; applied research may use qualitative, observational, experimental, or mixed methods.

## Nursing examples
A survey describing barriers to prenatal visits is descriptive; testing a new discharge-education approach is applied and may be experimental or quasi-experimental; exploring patients' lived experience after diagnosis may be qualitative and exploratory.

## PNLE high-yield
Identify the study's primary purpose from the objective, not just its title. Purpose alone does not tell you whether sampling is random, whether causality is supported, or whether ethics review is required.
