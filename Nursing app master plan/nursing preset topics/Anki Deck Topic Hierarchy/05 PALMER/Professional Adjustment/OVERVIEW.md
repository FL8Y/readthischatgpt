This section covers professional ethics, patient rights, confidentiality, consent, negligence, and tort concepts. Legal concepts must be interpreted under current Philippine law; foreign frameworks may explain terminology but are not controlling Philippine authority.

## PNLE anchors
Respect capable patients' informed choices, maintain confidentiality within lawful limits, document objectively, protect patients first after an incident, and escalate unsafe orders or practices. Do not equate an adverse outcome automatically with malpractice.

## Philippine context
Consult RA 9173, current PRC/BON Code of Ethics and standards, RA 10173 where privacy is involved, applicable laws/jurisprudence, and institutional policy.
