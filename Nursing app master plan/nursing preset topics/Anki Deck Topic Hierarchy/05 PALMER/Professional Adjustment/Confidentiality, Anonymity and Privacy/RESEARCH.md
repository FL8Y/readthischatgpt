# Confidentiality, Anonymity, and Privacy — PNLE Research

## Definitions
- **Privacy:** a person's interest in controlling access to their body, personal space, and personal information.
- **Confidentiality:** a professional duty to protect information entrusted during care and disclose it only with valid authority or a lawful basis.
- **Anonymity:** identity is not known or cannot reasonably be linked to the data; coded/pseudonymized data are not necessarily anonymous if a re-identification key exists.

## Safe nursing practice
- Discuss patients only with authorized people who need the information for care or another lawful purpose.
- Verify identity and authority before releasing records or giving information by phone or electronically.
- Use approved systems; lock screens, secure devices, avoid public conversations, and follow retention/disposal rules.
- Do not post patient stories, photos, screenshots, or “de-identified” details on social media without lawful authorization and appropriate consent.
- Collect only necessary information and use it only for a legitimate purpose.
- In research, explain data use, access, storage, retention, sharing, and limits of confidentiality in the consent process.

## Exceptions and escalation
Confidentiality may be limited by valid consent, applicable law, mandatory reporting, court process, or a serious safety circumstance recognized by law/policy. Disclose only what is necessary, to the proper recipient, and document the legal/policy basis. Do not independently invent an exception; consult privacy officers, supervisors, or legal/ethics resources when uncertain.

## Philippine context
RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its implementing rules establish obligations for processing personal information, alongside sectoral rules and professional confidentiality duties. Health information is sensitive personal information. Follow National Privacy Commission guidance, current law, PRC/BON standards, and facility policy.

## Commonly confused
- Coded data are not automatically anonymous.
- A family relationship alone does not automatically authorize access to all information of a capable adult.
- “For education” or “for research” does not by itself authorize disclosure.
- A breach should be reported promptly through organizational privacy/security procedures; do not hide it.
