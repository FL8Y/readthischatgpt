# Hypothesis — Research B

A hypothesis is a testable prediction about a difference, association, or effect. It must align with the question, variables, design, and planned analysis.

## Types
- **Null (H0):** commonly states no difference/association/effect.
- **Alternative (H1/Ha):** states a difference, association, or effect.
- **Directional/one-tailed:** predicts direction.
- **Non-directional/two-tailed:** predicts a difference without specifying direction.
Qualitative exploratory research often uses research questions rather than formal statistical hypotheses.

## Testing
The p-value is the probability, assuming the null model and its assumptions, of data at least as incompatible with H0 as observed. It is not the probability that H0 is true. If p is below the prespecified alpha, reject H0; otherwise fail to reject H0, not “prove it true.” Report effect size and confidence intervals.

## Errors
Type I error: rejecting a true H0. Type II error: failing to reject a false H0. Power is 1 − beta under specified assumptions.
