# Definitions in the Research Process

- **Problem statement:** concise description of the issue/gap and why it matters.
- **Research question:** focused question guiding the inquiry.
- **Aim/objective:** intended overall result or specific measurable steps.
- **Literature review:** critical synthesis of existing evidence.
- **Conceptual/theoretical framework:** concepts and relationships guiding interpretation.
- **Hypothesis:** testable prediction, usually in quantitative hypothesis testing.
- **Research design:** overall plan linking question, sampling, collection, and analysis.
- **Protocol:** prespecified procedures and safeguards for conducting the study.
- **Operational definition:** explicit specification of how a concept/variable is measured or categorized.
- **Sampling frame:** list/mechanism from which units are selected.
- **Data collection:** systematic acquisition of observations/records.
- **Data analysis:** procedures used to summarize or infer from data.
- **Results:** findings as analyzed.
- **Discussion:** interpretation, comparison with evidence, implications, and limitations.
- **Dissemination:** sharing findings responsibly with relevant audiences.
