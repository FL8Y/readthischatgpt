# Major Steps of the Research Process

## Typical sequence
1. Identify and refine the research problem.
2. Review and synthesize literature.
3. Develop the question, objectives, and framework/hypotheses where appropriate.
4. Select a design and methods.
5. Define population, sampling, sample size, and recruitment.
6. Select/develop tools and operational definitions; pilot as appropriate.
7. Obtain REC/ethics approval and other permissions before activities requiring approval.
8. Collect data according to protocol.
9. Clean, analyze, and interpret data.
10. Report findings, limitations, and implications transparently.
11. Disseminate and consider implementation/monitoring where appropriate.

## Important cautions
The process is not always strictly linear; qualitative designs may iterate between sampling, collection, and analysis. Ethics review must occur before relevant recruitment/data collection. Protocol changes may require approval. Do not fabricate or selectively omit results.
