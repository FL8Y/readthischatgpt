# Population and Sampling — Research B

## Core terms
Target population is the group to which the study aims to apply; accessible population is available for recruitment; sample is the group studied; sampling frame is the list/mechanism for selection. Inclusion/exclusion criteria should be scientifically justified.

## Methods
Probability methods include simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster, and multistage sampling. Non-probability methods include convenience, purposive, quota, snowball, and consecutive sampling. Select a method based on question, access, feasibility, and intended inference.

## Sample size and bias
Justify size using primary outcome, expected effect/precision, variability, alpha, power, design effect, and attrition. Large samples do not eliminate selection bias, nonresponse, confounding, or invalid measurement.

## Reporting
Describe setting, eligibility, recruitment, sample-size rationale, refusals/nonresponse, participant characteristics, and limitations. Probability sampling does not guarantee representativeness of the final responding sample.

## Ethics
Recruit fairly and voluntarily; protect privacy; minimize burden; and use appropriate safeguards for vulnerable participants.
