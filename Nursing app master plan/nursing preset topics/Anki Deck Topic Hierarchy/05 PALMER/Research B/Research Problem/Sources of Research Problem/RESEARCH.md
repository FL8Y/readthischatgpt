# Sources of Research Problems — Nursing Research

## Common sources
- **Clinical practice:** recurring complications, care delays, patient-safety events, adherence barriers, or variation in outcomes.
- **Literature review:** gaps, contradictory findings, under-studied groups, limitations, and calls for replication.
- **Theory:** untested relationships or questions about how a model applies in a new context.
- **Policy and technology changes:** new guidance, service models, or digital tools requiring evaluation.
- **Community/patient priorities:** lived experience, unmet needs, access barriers, and health inequities.
- **Quality data:** trends in falls, infections, readmissions, missed care, or patient experience.
- **Professional observation:** questions arising from education, leadership, or clinical workflow.

## Screening a potential problem
Check whether the question is novel enough to matter, answerable, feasible, ethically acceptable, and relevant to Philippine practice. Review existing evidence before assuming that a local observation is a general problem.
