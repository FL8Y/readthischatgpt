# Definitions in Nursing Research — Research B

- **Research problem:** gap, uncertainty, or practical issue requiring systematic inquiry.
- **Research question:** focused, answerable question guiding the study.
- **Objective/aim:** stated purpose or intended accomplishment.
- **Hypothesis:** testable prediction about a relationship/difference/effect.
- **Concept/construct:** abstract idea; a construct is developed for theoretical/scientific use.
- **Variable:** characteristic that can take different values.
- **Operational definition:** explicit description of how a concept/variable is observed or measured.
- **Population:** target group to which the study seeks relevance/generalization.
- **Sample:** participants/units actually studied.
- **Sampling frame:** list or mechanism used to select participants.
- **Instrument/tool:** measure or protocol used to collect data.
- **Validity:** evidence supporting intended interpretation/use of measurement or inference.
- **Reliability:** consistency under specified conditions.
- **Bias:** systematic distortion in selection, measurement, analysis, or interpretation.
- **Confounder:** factor that can distort an exposure-outcome association.
- **Generalizability:** applicability of findings to a broader target population; depends on design and context.
- **Transferability:** reader's assessment of relevance to another context, often discussed in qualitative research.
