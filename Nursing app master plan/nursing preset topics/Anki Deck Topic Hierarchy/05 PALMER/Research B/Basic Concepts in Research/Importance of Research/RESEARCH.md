# Importance of Nursing Research — Research B

Nursing research builds and evaluates evidence for safe, effective, equitable, patient-centered care. It can identify unmet needs, assess interventions, improve workflow, inform education and staffing, explain patient experiences, and guide policy.

## Practical contributions
- Supports evidence-based practice by integrating research evidence, clinical expertise, and patient preferences/context.
- Evaluates benefits, harms, implementation barriers, and outcomes.
- Identifies inequities and context-specific needs, including Philippine populations and health systems.
- Improves quality and safety when findings are translated, implemented, and monitored.
- Develops nursing knowledge and theories.

## Limits
One study rarely settles a question. Assess design, bias, precision, relevance, conflicts, and consistency with other evidence. Research evidence should be combined with professional judgment and patient values; guidelines require contextual application.

## Research vs quality improvement
Research often aims to produce generalizable knowledge; QI aims to improve a local process. The distinction depends on purpose and methods and is not always obvious. Consult the institution/REC rather than assuming an activity is exempt from review.
