# Basic Concepts in Research — Research B

## Research
Research is a systematic, transparent inquiry designed to answer a question using appropriate methods and evidence. In nursing, it informs practice, education, management, policy, and patient-safety improvement.

## Building blocks
- **Problem/question:** focused issue requiring investigation.
- **Literature review:** critical synthesis of existing evidence.
- **Framework/theory:** concepts and relationships guiding inquiry.
- **Aim/objectives:** what the study intends to accomplish.
- **Variables/concepts:** phenomena measured or explored.
- **Design/methods:** plan for sampling, data collection, and analysis.
- **Population/sample:** intended group and selected participants.
- **Findings/conclusions:** results interpreted within limitations.

## Quality principles
A sound study has a worthwhile question, suitable design, valid measurements, transparent sampling and analysis, ethical safeguards, and conclusions proportionate to evidence. Research methods must match the question; a large sample cannot correct systematic bias.

## Philippine context
Human health research should follow applicable PHREB ethical guidance, institutional REC requirements, privacy law, and relevant DOH/PRC rules.
