# Research Data Analysis — Research B

## Purpose and alignment
Analysis turns collected data into findings that answer the research question. The plan should align with design, outcomes, measurement levels, sampling, and protocol; specify primary analyses in advance when possible.

## Core steps
Validate and clean data transparently; describe sample and missingness; inspect distributions and assumptions; select suitable descriptive/inferential methods; estimate effect size and uncertainty; conduct justified sensitivity analyses; interpret in context; report all material findings including null or unfavorable results.

## Descriptive and inferential
Descriptive methods summarize the observed data. Inferential methods estimate population parameters or evaluate hypotheses under stated assumptions. A p-value is not the probability the null hypothesis is true, and statistical significance does not establish clinical importance or causality.

## Integrity and ethics
Protect confidentiality, restrict access, retain audit trails, document exclusions and transformations, and avoid selective reporting. Analysis choices made after seeing results should be identified as exploratory.
