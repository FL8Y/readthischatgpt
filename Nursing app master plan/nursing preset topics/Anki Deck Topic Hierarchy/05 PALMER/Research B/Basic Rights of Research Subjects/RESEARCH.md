# Basic Rights of Research Participants — PNLE Research

## Core rights and protections
Human research participants should receive respect, fair selection, understandable information, voluntary participation, appropriate privacy/confidentiality protections, and safeguards proportionate to risk. They should be able to ask questions and withdraw without improper penalty, subject to clearly explained limits and data already used under approved rules.

## Informed consent
Explain purpose, procedures, duration, risks, potential benefits, alternatives where relevant, compensation/injury arrangements if applicable, confidentiality limits, and contacts for questions/complaints. Avoid coercion or undue influence, especially when participants depend on the recruiter for care, grades, employment, or benefits.

## Vulnerability and justice
Children, persons with impaired decision-making capacity, economically disadvantaged groups, and dependent populations may need additional safeguards. Vulnerability does not automatically mean exclusion; recruitment should be fair and scientifically justified.

## Privacy and safety
Collect only necessary information, secure records, limit access, and disclose data only under authorized conditions. Monitor adverse events and report unanticipated problems to the REC/sponsor as required. Ethics review does not remove the researcher's ongoing responsibility.

## Philippine context
Follow the current PHREB National Ethical Guidelines, institutional REC requirements, RA 10173 Data Privacy Act, and applicable DOH rules. Verify current requirements for the specific study.
