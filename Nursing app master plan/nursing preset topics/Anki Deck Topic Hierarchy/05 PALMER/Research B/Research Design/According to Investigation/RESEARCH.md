# Research Design According to Investigation — Research B

Research taxonomies vary; “according to investigation” is not a universally standardized label. Interpret the folder in the context of the course's classification and distinguish the actual methods rather than forcing a single taxonomy.

## Common investigation approaches
- **Descriptive:** describes characteristics or frequency.
- **Exploratory:** investigates a poorly understood issue and develops concepts/questions.
- **Correlational/analytic:** evaluates relationships among variables.
- **Explanatory:** investigates possible mechanisms or explanations; causal strength depends on design.
- **Experimental/interventional:** assigns an intervention to evaluate outcomes.
- **Qualitative inquiry:** explores meaning, experience, context, or process.
- **Evaluation research:** assesses programs/services against criteria.

These categories overlap; purpose, design, and approach are separate dimensions. A study can be exploratory and qualitative, or evaluative and quasi-experimental.

## PNLE high-yield
Classify a study from its question, procedures, sampling, and analysis—not from its title alone. Avoid claiming causation when the design only supports description or association.
