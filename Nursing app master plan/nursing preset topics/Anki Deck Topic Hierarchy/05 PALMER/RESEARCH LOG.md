## Research requirements used for each topic
- Explain relevant foundations, definitions, classifications, principles, mechanisms, and terminology.
- Explain application, nursing responsibilities, rationales, monitoring, education, and evaluation.
- Include risks, red flags, exceptions, misconceptions, and comparisons where relevant.
- Cover medication contraindications, interactions, adverse effects, precautions, withholding/escalation criteria, and population-specific considerations when relevant.
- Apply ABCs, Maslow, actual-versus-potential problems, acute-versus-chronic risk, and the nursing process where appropriate; do not apply frameworks mechanically.
- Separate Philippine law/regulatory requirements from international guidance.
- Cite source title, author/organization, date/version when available, and direct link. Do not claim a source was checked unless it was.
- Do not include practice questions or quizzes.
