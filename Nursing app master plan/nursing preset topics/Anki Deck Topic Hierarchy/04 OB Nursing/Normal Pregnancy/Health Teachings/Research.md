# Pregnancy Health Teachings: PNLE Nursing Review

## Core goals
Pregnancy teaching should be individualized, evidence-based and culturally respectful. Assess what the patient already knows, barriers to care, language needs and preferences. Use teach-back rather than relying on “Do you understand?”

## Nutrition and supplements
- Encourage a varied, balanced diet and appropriate gestational weight gain based on pre-pregnancy BMI and clinician/dietitian advice.
- Folic acid is recommended before conception and in early pregnancy; dose depends on baseline risk and current local guidance.
- Iron/folate and other supplements should follow current antenatal protocol and laboratory/risk assessment.
- Avoid high-risk foodborne exposures; follow local advice on thoroughly cooking meat/eggs/seafood, safe food handling and avoiding unpasteurized products.
- Avoid alcohol during pregnancy; advise avoiding tobacco/nicotine and non-prescribed drugs.
- Caffeine advice varies by guideline; follow current local recommendations and discuss total intake from all sources.

## Activity, rest and daily safety
- Encourage safe physical activity for uncomplicated pregnancy unless contraindicated; adapt to baseline fitness and clinical condition.
- Discuss sleep, posture, oral health, hydration, seat-belt use and avoiding overheating/activities with high fall or abdominal-trauma risk.
- Review occupational and environmental exposures.
- Do not recommend bed rest routinely without a specific indication; it may have harms and should be clinician-directed.

## Medication and infection prevention
- Review prescription medicines, OTC drugs, herbal products and supplements with a qualified clinician/pharmacist before starting or stopping.
- Keep vaccines current according to Philippine recommendations and individualized clinical advice.
- Teach hand hygiene, food safety, avoidance of known infection exposures and prompt evaluation for fever or concerning symptoms.
- Do not assume “natural” products are safe in pregnancy.

## Antenatal visits and screening
Explain the purpose of visits and recommended tests, including dating, blood pressure, blood/urine testing, infection screening, diabetes screening, fetal growth and ultrasound as indicated. Screening schedules and test panels vary; use current DOH/facility guidance.

## Danger signs and when to seek care
Seek urgent maternity assessment for vaginal bleeding, fluid leakage, severe or persistent abdominal pain, regular preterm contractions, reduced fetal movement, severe headache/visual changes, epigastric/RUQ pain, seizure, fever, chest pain, severe dyspnea, syncope, persistent vomiting/dehydration, or unilateral leg swelling/pain. Provide the correct local emergency contact and referral location.

## Birth preparedness
Discuss planned birth location, transport, emergency contacts, support person, finances, blood availability where relevant, newborn feeding, postpartum recovery, contraception and mental-health support. Reassess the plan as risk and circumstances change.

## Nursing approach
Use plain language, teach-back, written reminders and shared decision-making. Avoid blame or stigma around weight, substance use, mental health or missed appointments; identify practical barriers and connect the patient to support.

## PNLE high-yield points
- Teach red flags explicitly; routine antenatal education does not replace urgent evaluation.
- Medication review includes OTC/herbal products and supplements.
- Folic acid timing is important; high-risk dosing must follow clinician guidance.
- Avoid alcohol, tobacco/nicotine and non-prescribed drugs.
- Do not prescribe bed rest or activity restriction reflexively.

## Philippine practice considerations
Use current DOH antenatal-care policies, local immunization recommendations, facility referral pathways and PRC/BON scope-of-practice standards. International guidance should not be presented as a Philippine legal requirement unless officially adopted.

Supplement doses, vaccination timing and screening schedules are risk- and policy-dependent. Confirm current Philippine DOH/facility guidance before giving a specific regimen.
