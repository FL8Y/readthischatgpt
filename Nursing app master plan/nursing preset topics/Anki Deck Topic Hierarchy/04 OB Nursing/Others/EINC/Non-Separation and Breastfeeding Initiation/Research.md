# Non-Separation and Early Breastfeeding Initiation: PNLE Nursing Review

## Core principle
For clinically stable mother-newborn pairs, maintain uninterrupted skin-to-skin contact and support breastfeeding initiation within the first hour after birth. Non-separation promotes thermal stability, bonding, feeding cues, colostrum intake and breastfeeding continuation. Routine weighing, bathing and non-urgent procedures should not interrupt this time-sensitive period when they can safely wait.

## Breastfeeding initiation
- Observe newborn readiness cues such as rooting, hand-to-mouth activity, alertness and mouthing.
- Support positioning and deep latch; do not force the newborn to latch or force-feed.
- Explain that colostrum is produced in small volumes and is concentrated in immune and protective factors.
- Assess latch, suck/swallow, maternal comfort and infant feeding behavior; provide practical assistance and respectful coaching.
- Support exclusive breastfeeding unless there is a medical indication for supplementation or the informed parent chooses another feeding plan.

## Safe non-separation
Maintain skin-to-skin with the newborn's airway visible and unobstructed. Observe the infant's breathing, color, tone and temperature, and assess maternal alertness and stability. If either needs urgent care, separate only as clinically necessary, provide safe care, communicate with the family, and reunite promptly when stable. If the mother is sedated or unable to safely hold the newborn, a trained staff member should ensure thermal safety and supervision.

## Nursing actions and rationale
| Action | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Support immediate and uninterrupted contact for stable dyads | Helps thermoregulation and feeding initiation. |
| Recognize cues and assist with latch | Improves effective early feeding without coercion. |
| Delay routine procedures that are safe to postpone | Reduces avoidable separation and missed early feeding. |
| Assess airway and maternal alertness | Prevents unsafe positioning or unattended infant risk. |
| Document feeding initiation, latch/support and reasons for separation | Supports continuity and quality monitoring. |
| Provide expressing support if separation is unavoidable | Maintains milk production and provides breast milk when appropriate. |

## Exceptions and safety
Resuscitation, maternal hemorrhage/instability, newborn respiratory distress, significant illness or another urgent clinical need takes priority over uninterrupted contact. Follow neonatal resuscitation and maternal emergency protocols. Do not delay lifesaving care to complete an EINC checklist.

## PNLE high-yield points
- Early initiation means breastfeeding within the first hour when mother and newborn are stable and ready.
- Colostrum is beneficial; small volume is physiologic.
- Non-separation is not “no monitoring”: airway, breathing, temperature and maternal status still require observation.
- If separation is medically necessary, explain the reason and reunite/support milk expression as soon as safe.

## Philippine practice context
EINC/Unang Yakap is promoted by the Philippine DOH with WHO support. Follow current DOH, Baby-Friendly Hospital Initiative and institutional policies for rooming-in, feeding support, newborn procedures and documented exceptions.
