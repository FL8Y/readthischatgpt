# Calcium Imbalance: Hypocalcemia and Hypercalcemia

## Calcium physiology
Calcium is essential for neuromuscular transmission, muscle contraction, coagulation and bone integrity. Total calcium depends partly on albumin; ionized calcium reflects the biologically active fraction. Albumin, pH, renal function, magnesium, phosphate, parathyroid hormone and vitamin D influence interpretation.

## Hypocalcemia
Causes include hypoparathyroidism/post-thyroid surgery, vitamin D deficiency, CKD, pancreatitis, hypomagnesemia and selected medications. Findings include perioral/finger tingling, cramps, tetany, hyperreflexia, positive Chvostek/Trousseau signs, seizures and QT prolongation. Check total and/or ionized calcium, albumin, magnesium, phosphate, renal function and cause-directed studies. Severe symptoms or arrhythmias are emergencies. IV calcium may be prescribed for severe symptomatic cases with ECG monitoring; correct magnesium when indicated.

## Hypercalcemia
Causes include hyperparathyroidism, malignancy, vitamin D toxicity, immobilization and some medicines. Findings include thirst/polyuria, dehydration, constipation, nausea, weakness, kidney stones, confusion and coma in severe cases. Assess ECG, renal function, hydration, ionized/total calcium and PTH according to context. Treatment is severity/cause-specific and may involve hydration if safe, antiresorptive agents for malignancy-related disease, calcitonin in selected acute cases or dialysis in severe/refractory situations. Fluid therapy must be individualized in heart/renal failure.

## Nursing priorities
- New seizure, tetany with airway risk, altered consciousness, severe dehydration, arrhythmia or rapidly rising calcium requires urgent escalation.
- Monitor ECG when clinically indicated, intake/output, renal function, calcium, magnesium and phosphate.
- Assess post-thyroid/parathyroid surgery for perioral numbness, tingling, muscle spasm or stridor (possible acute hypocalcemia).
- Avoid generic supplement or diet recommendations until cause/labs and kidney status are known.
- Teach fall precautions when weakness/confusion occurs and medication adherence as prescribed.

## PNLE distinctions
- Hypocalcemia increases neuromuscular excitability and can prolong QT; hypercalcemia often causes weakness/constipation/polyuria and may shorten QT.
- Total calcium may be misleading when albumin is abnormal; use ionized calcium when indicated.
- Post-neck surgery airway symptoms are an emergency.
