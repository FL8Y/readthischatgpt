# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

**Status:** Researched draft; source and clinical audit pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Definition and complications
CKD is kidney structural/function abnormality persisting at least 3 months with health implications. Classification uses cause, GFR category and albuminuria. Complications include fluid overload, hypertension, anemia, hyperkalemia, metabolic acidosis, mineral/bone disorder and uremia.
## Assessment
Trend eGFR, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, BP, weight, electrolytes, bicarbonate, hemoglobin, calcium/phosphate and symptoms. Assess edema, dyspnea, pruritus, nausea, fatigue, cognition and medication use.
## Management and nursing
Control BP/diabetes, reduce cardiovascular risk, avoid nephrotoxins, review dose adjustments, provide individualized nutrition and prepare for kidney replacement therapy when indicated. ACEi/ARB and SGLT2 inhibitors are recommended for selected CKD groups but require assessment of potassium, kidney function, volume status and contraindications. Monitor for initial expected eGFR changes and clinically important adverse effects according to guideline.
## Emergency signs
Refractory hyperkalemia, pulmonary edema, severe acidosis, pericarditis/encephalopathy from uremia or rapid deterioration need urgent evaluation.
## Patient education
Medication adherence, BP/glucose control, smoking cessation, individualized sodium/protein/potassium/phosphate guidance and avoidance of unreviewed OTC NSAIDs. Do not prescribe a universal potassium/protein restriction without labs and dietitian plan.
## PNLE traps
CKD staging requires chronicity and eGFR/albuminuria, not a single creatinine. Dialysis is initiated for clinical indications/complications, not solely an eGFR number.
