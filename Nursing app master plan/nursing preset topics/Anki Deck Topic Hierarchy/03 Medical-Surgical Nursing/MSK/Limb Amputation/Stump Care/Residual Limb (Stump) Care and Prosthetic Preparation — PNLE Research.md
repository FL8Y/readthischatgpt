# Residual Limb (Stump) Care and Prosthetic Preparation

**Status:** Draft researched under the updated full research standard; source-to-claim audit and file reopen check pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Goals and assessment
Residual-limb care supports wound healing, edema control, shaping, skin integrity and eventual prosthetic use. Assess incision, drainage, temperature, color, sensation, swelling, pain, pressure areas and fit of dressings/devices.
## Care principles
Follow surgical instructions for dressing changes and compression/shrinker use. Keep skin clean/dry and inspect daily once permitted; do not apply lotions to an unhealed incision unless directed. Use prescribed wrapping technique, ensuring even pressure without constriction. Position to reduce contracture risk and perform ROM/strengthening under rehabilitation guidance.
## Complications
Escalate fever, purulent drainage, spreading erythema, wound separation, increasing pain, cool/pale residual limb or uncontrolled edema. Prosthesis use requires adequate healing and specialist fitting; avoid self-adjusting a painful prosthesis without assessment.
## Psychosocial and function
Support desensitization and gradual contact only when cleared, phantom pain treatment, mobility training and realistic prosthetic expectations. Teach fall prevention and safe transfers.
## PNLE traps
Too-tight wrapping can impair circulation; uneven wrapping can cause edema. Inspect skin frequently, especially with diabetes or neuropathy.
