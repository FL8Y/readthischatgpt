# Preoperative Nursing Care for Limb Amputation

**Status:** Draft researched under the updated full research standard; source-to-claim audit and file reopen check pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Assessment and preparation
Assess indication, vascular status, infection, pain, comorbidities, nutrition, glycemic control, smoking, mobility, coping, home environment and support. Verify consent, procedure/side, allergies, medication plan, labs and fasting instructions according to policy.
## Education
Explain expected postoperative pain, residual-limb shaping, positioning, mobility aids, rehabilitation and prosthetic pathway. Include realistic discussion of phantom sensation/pain and body image. Encourage patient questions and involve family/support persons with consent.
## Safety
Optimize skin/infection status and vascular risk as directed. Anticoagulant, antiplatelet, insulin and other medication changes must follow authorized perioperative orders; do not independently stop medicines. Confirm correct site marking and surgical checklist.
## Nursing priorities
Address anxiety and informed participation while identifying urgent ischemia, uncontrolled infection or unstable comorbidity. Coordinate referrals early to rehabilitation and social services.
## PNLE traps
Preoperative teaching should include rehabilitation and function, not only surgery. Medication withholding requires an explicit plan and reconciliation.
