# De Quervain Tenosynovitis

**Status:** Draft researched under the updated full research standard; source-to-claim audit and file reopen check pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Definition and findings
De Quervain tenosynovitis is painful inflammation/thickening of tendon sheaths around the abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis at the radial wrist. Pain worsens with thumb movement, grasping, lifting or ulnar deviation; swelling/tenderness may occur near the radial styloid.
## Assessment and differential
Assess onset, repetitive thumb/wrist use, postpartum caregiving, trauma, swelling and function. Distinguish from thumb carpometacarpal arthritis, scaphoid injury, intersection syndrome and nerve disorders. A provocative maneuver can reproduce symptoms but should be interpreted with history/exam.
## Management
Activity modification, thumb-spica splinting, selected NSAIDs/topical analgesia and corticosteroid injection may be used. NSAIDs require patient-specific renal, GI, cardiovascular, pregnancy and anticoagulant checks. Persistent symptoms may require surgical release.
## Nursing care and teaching
Teach avoidance of aggravating grips/lifts, safe infant-lifting technique if relevant, splint use and follow-up. After injection or surgery, monitor pain, skin, infection, sensation and function; worsening numbness or severe swelling requires review.
## PNLE traps
Radial-sided wrist pain with thumb motion suggests De Quervain, but trauma/scaphoid fracture must not be missed. Do not assume all wrist pain is carpal tunnel.
