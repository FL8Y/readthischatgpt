## Mission and quality gate

Research every topic in its existing location. A topic is **not complete because a folder or file exists**. Mark a topic verified only after the content has been checked for clinical accuracy, cited sources have been opened and matched to claims, medication/emergency safety has been reviewed where applicable, and the uploaded file has been reopened from GitHub.

Required topic coverage, as applicable: definition/classification; pathophysiology; causes and risk factors; manifestations and assessment; diagnostics and laboratory interpretation; treatments; medication indications, contraindications, interactions, adverse effects and nursing responsibilities; nursing interventions with rationales; complications/red flags; patient education; PNLE priorities and distractor traps; Philippine-specific practice context; verifiable references.
