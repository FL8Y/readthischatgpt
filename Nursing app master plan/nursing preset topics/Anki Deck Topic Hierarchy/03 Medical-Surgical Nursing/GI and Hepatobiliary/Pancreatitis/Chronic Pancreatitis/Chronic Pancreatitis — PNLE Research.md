# Chronic Pancreatitis

**Status:** Substantive research draft. Source-to-claim and Philippine-context verification pending. Reviewed 2026-10-17.

## Core concepts, assessment, management and nursing care
Chronic pancreatitis is persistent inflammation causing fibrosis and irreversible loss of exocrine/endocrine function. Risks include alcohol, tobacco, recurrent acute pancreatitis, genetic and obstructive causes. Findings include epigastric pain radiating to the back, weight loss, steatorrhea, malnutrition and diabetes; lipase can be normal in advanced disease. Diagnosis may use CT/MRI/MRCP, endoscopic ultrasound or functional tests. Management includes alcohol/tobacco cessation, nutrition support, pancreatic enzyme replacement with meals/snacks for exocrine insufficiency, fat-soluble vitamin assessment and diabetes care. Teach correct enzyme timing and monitor weight, stool, pain and glucose. Escalate new severe pain, persistent vomiting or jaundice. Analgesics, including opioids when selected, require monitoring for sedation, constipation and respiratory depression. Sources: NIDDK pancreatitis https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/pancreatitis ; Merck Manual chronic pancreatitis https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/gastrointestinal-disorders/pancreatitis/chronic-pancreatitis
