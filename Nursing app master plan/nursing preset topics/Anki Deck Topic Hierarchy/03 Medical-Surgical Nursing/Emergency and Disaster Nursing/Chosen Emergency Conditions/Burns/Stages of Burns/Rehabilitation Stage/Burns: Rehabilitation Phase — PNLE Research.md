# Burns: Rehabilitation Phase

**Status:** Draft researched under the updated full research standard; source-to-claim audit and file reopen check pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Goals
Rehabilitation begins as acute care stabilizes and may continue for months/years. Goals include functional recovery, scar/contracture management, psychosocial adjustment, reintegration and prevention of disability.
## Nursing interventions
Coordinate range-of-motion, positioning, splinting, pressure garments or scar treatment as ordered by the burn team. Encourage graded activity and self-care while managing pain and fatigue. Assess itch, sleep, mood, body image, trauma symptoms, family support and access barriers.
## Skin and scar safety
Healed burn skin may be fragile, dry, sensitive and vulnerable to sun injury. Teach moisturization and sun protection according to specialist guidance, gradual stretching, adherence to pressure garments if prescribed, and early reporting of breakdown or contracture.
## Psychosocial and discharge care
Set realistic goals, support return to school/work and community, address stigma and mental health, and provide follow-up for reconstructive needs. Teach wound/skin care and emergency signs.
## PNLE traps
Rehabilitation is an active treatment phase, not merely discharge. Preventing contracture and restoring function require early coordinated planning.
