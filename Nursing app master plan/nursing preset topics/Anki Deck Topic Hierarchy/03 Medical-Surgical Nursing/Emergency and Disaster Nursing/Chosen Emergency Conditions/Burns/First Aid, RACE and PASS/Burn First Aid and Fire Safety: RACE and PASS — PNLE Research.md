# Burn First Aid and Fire Safety: RACE and PASS

**Status:** Draft researched under the updated full research standard; source-to-claim audit and file reopen check pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Burn first aid
Ensure rescuer safety, stop the burning process, remove jewelry/tight items before swelling, and cool thermal burns with cool running water when feasible. Avoid ice, grease, toothpaste and nonsterile home remedies. Cover with clean dry material and prevent hypothermia. For chemical burns, protect self, remove contaminated clothing and irrigate according to chemical-specific guidance. Electrical injuries require power isolation before contact and emergency evaluation.
## RACE fire response
RACE commonly means Rescue people in immediate danger, Alarm/activate fire response, Confine the fire by closing doors if safe, and Extinguish/evacuate according to training and local policy. Rescue must not put staff or others at unreasonable risk.
## PASS extinguisher use
PASS commonly means Pull the pin, Aim at the base, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Use only when trained, the fire is small/incipient, the correct extinguisher is available and an exit remains behind the user; evacuate and close doors if unsafe.
## Nursing priorities
Activate emergency response, assess airway/breathing and associated trauma, and follow facility fire/burn protocol. Smoke exposure, facial burns, hoarseness, soot or stridor may indicate evolving inhalation injury.
## PNLE traps
Scene safety comes first. Do not fight a fire when escape is threatened. RACE/PASS mnemonics do not override local fire policy or rescuer safety.
