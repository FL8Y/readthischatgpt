# Canadian Triage and Acuity Scale (CTAS): Overview

**Status:** Draft researched under the updated full research standard; source-to-claim audit and file reopen check pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Purpose and levels
CTAS is a five-level triage acuity scale used in Canadian emergency settings, ranging from resuscitation (Level I) to non-urgent (Level V). It incorporates presenting complaint, modifiers, vital signs, pain/distress and clinical judgment. Exact operational rules are set by the current CTAS guidance and local system.
## Nursing application
Perform a focused primary assessment, identify complaint-specific modifiers and high-risk signs, assign the appropriate category using current training, document rationale and reassess if symptoms/vitals change. Do not substitute a remembered list for the official algorithm.
## Limitations and context
CTAS is a Canadian system and should not be described as the national Philippine triage standard. Philippine facilities may use locally adopted protocols or another validated system; follow the institution's approved pathway.
## PNLE traps
Triage level represents urgency, not final diagnosis. A patient initially appearing stable can deteriorate; re-triage on change. Distinguish CTAS from ESI and local three-level systems.
