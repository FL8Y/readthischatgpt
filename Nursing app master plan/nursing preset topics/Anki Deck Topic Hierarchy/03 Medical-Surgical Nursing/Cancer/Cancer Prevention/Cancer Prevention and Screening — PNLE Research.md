# Cancer Prevention and Screening

**Status:** Researched draft; source and clinical audit pending. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-17.

## Prevention levels
Primary prevention reduces risk (tobacco avoidance, vaccination against HPV/HBV when indicated, UV protection, healthy weight/activity, limiting carcinogenic exposures). Secondary prevention detects selected cancers through evidence-based screening. Screening is for asymptomatic populations and differs from diagnostic evaluation of symptoms.
## Screening principles
Use current national/local guidance, age, risk, family history, prior results and patient preferences. Tests may include cervical screening, breast imaging, colorectal screening and lung CT for eligible high-risk people. A screening interval or eligibility from one country's guideline should not be presented as universal Philippine policy.
## Nursing responsibilities
Take family/personal history, explain benefits/harms (false positives, overdiagnosis, procedure risks), facilitate informed decisions and follow-up of abnormal results. Encourage HPV/HBV vaccination per current Philippine immunization guidance and eligible patient factors.
## Red flags
A new mass, unexplained bleeding, persistent symptom or suspicious lesion needs diagnostic assessment even if screening is not due or a previous screen was normal.
## PNLE traps
Screening does not diagnose cancer. Follow the specific age/risk guideline and local program; do not promise screening prevents all cancer.
