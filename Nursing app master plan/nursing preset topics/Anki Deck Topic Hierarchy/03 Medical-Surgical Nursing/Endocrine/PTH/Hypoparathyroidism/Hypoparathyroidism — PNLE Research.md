# Hypoparathyroidism

**Status:** Substantive research draft. Source-to-claim and Philippine-context verification pending. Reviewed 2026-10-17.

## Core concepts, assessment and nursing priorities
Low PTH causes hypocalcemia and often hyperphosphatemia; causes include parathyroid injury during neck surgery and autoimmune/genetic conditions. Symptoms include perioral/fingertip tingling, cramps, tetany, laryngospasm and seizures. Assess airway, neuromuscular irritability, calcium, phosphate, magnesium, renal function and ECG when indicated. Severe or symptomatic hypocalcemia may require monitored IV calcium; long-term care may include oral calcium and active vitamin D with monitoring for hypercalciuria and renal complications. After thyroid/parathyroid surgery report stridor, spasms, worsening tingling or seizure urgently. Sources: NIDDK https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypoparathyroidism ; Merck Manual https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/endocrine-and-metabolic-disorders/parathyroid-disorders/hypoparathyroidism

## PNLE priorities and safety
Prioritize airway, breathing, circulation and acute neurologic changes; use trends and patient presentation rather than isolated laboratory values. Follow current orders, local protocols and nursing scope. Explain unexpected deterioration promptly.
