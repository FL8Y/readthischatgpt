# Hyperthyroidism and Thyrotoxicosis

## Pathophysiology and causes
Hyperthyroidism refers to excess synthesis/secretion of thyroid hormone; thyrotoxicosis describes the clinical state of excess thyroid hormone from any cause. Graves disease, toxic nodules and thyroiditis are different mechanisms and are not treated identically. Excess thyroid hormone increases metabolic rate and adrenergic sensitivity.

## Assessment and diagnostics
Findings may include heat intolerance, sweating, weight loss despite appetite, tremor, anxiety, insomnia, frequent stools, tachycardia, atrial fibrillation, systolic hypertension, proximal muscle weakness and goiter. Graves disease can cause eye findings. Test TSH and free T4 (and sometimes T3) and investigate cause using antibodies/imaging when indicated. Results vary by pregnancy, illness and medicines.

## Thyroid storm
Thyroid storm is rare but life-threatening, often triggered by infection, surgery, trauma or untreated thyrotoxicosis. Suspect it with severe tachycardia/arrhythmia, fever, agitation/delirium, vomiting/diarrhea, heart failure or collapse. Activate emergency care; support ABCs, monitor ECG and temperature, treat precipitating illness and prepare ordered beta blockade, antithyroid therapy, iodine (after antithyroid drug timing), glucocorticoids and cooling/supportive care. Drug sequence and contraindications matter; do not independently initiate treatment.

## Nursing care and education
Monitor heart rate/rhythm, temperature, weight, hydration, stool pattern and sleep. Teach adherence and follow-up lab monitoring for antithyroid medicines; fever or sore throat while taking a thionamide can signal rare agranulocytosis and requires prompt medical evaluation. New jaundice or dark urine may require urgent medication review. Educate about symptoms of hypothyroidism if treatment overshoots. Radioiodine or surgery requires specific reproductive, radiation-safety or postoperative education.

## PNLE distinctions
- Hyperthyroidism often causes heat intolerance, weight loss, tremor and tachycardia; hypothyroidism tends toward cold intolerance, weight gain and bradycardia.
- Thyroid storm is a clinical emergency; do not treat as routine anxiety.
- Antithyroid medicines have important rare adverse effects; fever/sore throat requires escalation.
