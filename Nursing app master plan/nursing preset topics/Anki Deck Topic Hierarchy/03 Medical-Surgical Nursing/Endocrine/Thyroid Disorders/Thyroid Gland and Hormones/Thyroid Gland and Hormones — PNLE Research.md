## Core concepts, assessment, management and nursing care
The thyroid produces T4 and T3, regulating metabolism, heat production, cardiovascular function and growth. Pituitary TSH regulates thyroid synthesis; iodine is required. Primary thyroid dysfunction is usually assessed with TSH and free T4, but pregnancy, pituitary disease, acute illness and medicines alter interpretation. Hypothyroidism may cause fatigue, cold intolerance, constipation and bradycardia; hyperthyroidism may cause heat intolerance, tremor, weight loss and tachycardia. Levothyroxine should be taken consistently and separated from iron/calcium as instructed. Antithyroid drugs can rarely cause agranulocytosis or liver injury; fever/sore throat or jaundice requires urgent review. After thyroidectomy monitor airway, neck swelling, voice, swallowing and hypocalcemia. Stridor or rapidly expanding neck swelling is an emergency. Sources: American Thyroid Association https://www.thyroid.org/thyroid-function-tests/ ; NIDDK https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases
