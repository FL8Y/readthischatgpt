## 3. Added explanation and memory structure

### High-yield grouping
- **Maternal/child health and nutrition:** newborn screening, hearing screening, immunization, breastfeeding/rooming-in, salt iodization, food fortification, infant feeding, maternity leave.
- **Health system and workforce:** Nursing Act, national health insurance, public health worker benefits, local government responsibilities, FHSIS, UHC policy.
- **Noncommunicable-disease and substance regulation:** tobacco, dangerous drugs, traditional/alternative medicine, alcohol/tobacco excise taxation.
- **Communicable-disease control:** notification/surveillance, HIV, rabies, TB, leprosy, polio/immunization, dengue.
- **Environmental/disaster health:** sanitation, waste, water, air, FDA, building safety, Red Cross, disaster risk reduction.
- **Occupational health, privacy, and professional development:** Labor Code, OSHC, employee compensation, pesticides/toxic substances, child protection, CPD, data privacy, mental health policy.
- **Education/qualification and student participation:** PQF and National Student's Day.

### Distinctions worth remembering
1. A law title is not the same as its current status. This guide is a study list, not a consolidated current-law database.
2. The guide lists RA 3573, but RA 11332 (2019) expressly repealed it and now governs mandatory reporting of notifiable diseases and health events of public health concern.
3. The guide lists RA 8504 (1998), but RA 11166 (2018), the Philippine HIV and AIDS Policy Act, expressly repealed RA 8504.
4. RA 7600 is the original Rooming-In and Breastfeeding Act; RA 10028 expanded breastfeeding promotion and amended RA 7600.
5. RA 11210 is the 105-Day Expanded Maternity Leave Law. Details depend on eligibility and circumstances; use the law and implementing rules for precise application.
6. RA 9173 is the Philippine Nursing Act of 2002.
7. RA 10173 governs personal-information processing and data privacy; health information should not be treated as freely shareable simply because it is used in a health setting.

## 4. External legal cross-checks and corrections to the study guide

These are external cross-checks, not claims transcribed from the page.

- **RA 8504 is historical, not the current principal HIV statute:** RA 11166 expressly repealed RA 8504. For current HIV/AIDS law review, study RA 11166 alongside relevant current rules.
- **RA 3573 is historical, not the current reporting statute:** RA 11332 expressly repealed it. For current mandatory disease reporting, use RA 11332 and applicable DOH rules.
- **Breastfeeding legislation:** the Senate Legislative Reference Bureau identifies RA 10028 as an amendment to RA 7600; do not memorize them as unrelated statutes.
- **RA 6425:** this was the Dangerous Drugs Act of 1972; it was superseded/repealed by later dangerous-drug legislation, including RA 9165. The guide's phrasing “Penalties for Violations…” is not the full statute title.
- **RA 7846:** the guide's description refers to compulsory hepatitis B immunization. Treat the exact title/coverage as a legal reference to verify rather than relying on shortened wording.
- **RA 10352:** verify the number/title before memorizing. The guide's “Excise Tax on Alcohol & Tobacco” label may be shorthand or a transcription issue; do not infer the full statute title from it.
- **RA 8749:** the number is printed twice in the image; likely a duplication/printing artifact, not two separate laws.
- **RA 6541 / PD 1096:** the page groups these references together in the environmental section and repeats RA 6541 in the occupational-health section. Preserve the guide's listing, but verify exact legislative relationships before using it as a legal citation.
- **RA 1136:** the page labels it “Tuberculosis Law of 1954.” Preserve this source wording, but verify the law number/title against an authoritative legal source before relying on it.
