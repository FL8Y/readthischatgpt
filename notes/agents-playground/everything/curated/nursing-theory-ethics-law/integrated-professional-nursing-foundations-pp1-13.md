## 1. How these concepts fit together

| Foundation | What it contributes | What it does not replace |
|---|---|---|
| Law, regulation, and professional standards | Defines formal duties, legal frameworks, requirements, and authorized professional practice | Does not remove the need to assess the individual patient's situation or consult current authoritative law/policy |
| Nursing theory | Provides models for understanding the person, environment, health, relationships, self-care, and nursing goals | Does not by itself establish a legal rule or dictate one universal intervention |

## 2. Legal and health-legislation notes: use both source views

Two source notes overlap in topic and around printed page 3, but organize the material differently:

- **Laws Affecting Nursing Practice (images 1–3)** groups items by legal/administrative instrument type, including Board of Nursing resolutions, proclamations/pronouncements, letters of instruction, executive orders, presidential decrees, and Republic Acts.
- **Philippine Health Legislation (printed pp. 3–5)** groups listed laws by public-health topic, including maternal/child health, health service delivery, communicable and noncommunicable diseases, environmental/disaster health, occupational health, and additional instruments.

**Relationship classification:** Complementary notes with topic/page overlap, not confirmed duplicates. One is organized by type of instrument; the other by health area. Both are retained because their structure makes different kinds of recall easier.

### A safe study method for legislation

For each instrument, keep the **exact number, formal title, year, stated subject, source page, and verification status** together. Do not infer a law's current legal status from a photographed study-guide entry. The source notes include selective legal cross-checks; use their cited official sources for current status, and treat unresolved/uncross-checked entries as needing verification rather than silently “correcting” them.

## 4. Ethics and professional development

The pp. 11–13 source note connects universal moral values/principles in clinical nursing practice with Benner's five stages of clinical-skill development. Keep these concepts distinct:

- **Ethical principles** support reasoning about what should be considered in a decision (for example, autonomy, beneficence, nonmaleficence, and justice as detailed in the source).
- **Benner's stages** describe a model of how practical nursing skill/expertise develops over time.
- **Law and scope of practice** establish external requirements and boundaries; neither personal expertise nor an ethical rationale automatically permits an action outside authorized scope.

Use the source note for its exact definitions, examples, the ordered Benner stages, and source caveats. The summary above is explanatory linkage, not a replacement for its table or examples.

## 5. Integrated retrieval practice

1. Why keep both the “laws affecting nursing practice” note and the “health legislation pp. 3–5” note?  
   **Answer:** They organize related material differently—one by type of legal/administrative instrument and the other by public-health topic—and thus are complementary rather than confirmed duplicates.

2. What is the difference between a theory note and an ethics note?  
   **Answer:** A theory/model provides a framework for understanding nursing/person/environment/health or guiding care; an ethics note addresses principles and reasoning relevant to values and decisions. Their exact content and applicability are described in their own sources.

3. Why is the full Henderson list retained separately when Henderson also appears in the theorists table?  
   **Answer:** The standalone note preserves the complete fourteen-item sequence; the theorists note makes Henderson comparable with other models.

4. Why must Benner's skill stages not be treated as legal authorization?  
   **Answer:** A developmental/expertise model is not a substitute for current legal scope, credentialing, employer policy, or the requirements of a specific clinical task.

5. What should be done if a printed law number/title conflicts with a current official source?  
   **Answer:** Preserve what the photographed source says, identify the conflict separately, consult the relevant official authority, and document the date/status of the verification. Do not silently rewrite the source note.
