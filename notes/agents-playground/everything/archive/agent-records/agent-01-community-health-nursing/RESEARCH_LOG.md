## Important source discrepancies retained, not silently corrected
- The textbook labels maternal mortality as “MMR” and shows a multiplier of 1,000. WHO's standard maternal mortality ratio is expressed per 100,000 live births. The notes preserve the textbook formula and separately explain the external convention.
- The textbook's FDR formula uses registered live births alone in the denominator. CDC/NCHS fetal mortality definitions use live births plus fetal deaths meeting a specified gestational-age threshold. Both conventions are distinguished.
- The page's wording for Pearson's r is summarized as relationship between two continuous variables; added explanation specifies linear association and cautions that correlation does not prove causation.
