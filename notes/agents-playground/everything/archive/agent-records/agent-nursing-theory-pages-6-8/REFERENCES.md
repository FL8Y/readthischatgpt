## External sources consulted

1. American Nurses Association, “What Is Nursing Theory?” (July 5, 2023): https://www.nursingworld.org/content-hub/resources/becoming-a-nurse/nursing-theory/  
   General context for the role of nursing theory, clinical reasoning, and the nursing metaparadigm.

2. OpenStax, *Fundamentals of Nursing*, “1.4 History and Evolution of Nursing Theories”: https://openstax.org/books/fundamentals-nursing/pages/1-4-history-and-evolution-of-nursing-theories  
   Broad historical context for Nightingale, Henderson, Peplau, Orem, Neuman, and Leininger.

3. OpenStax, *Fundamentals of Nursing*, “1.5 Selected Nursing Theorist”: https://openstax.org/books/fundamentals-nursing/pages/1-5-selected-nursing-theorist  
   Introductory context on nursing theorists' role in practice, education, and research.

4. Nursing-Theory.org, “Virginia Henderson's Contribution to Nursing Theory: Nursing Need Theory”: https://nursingtheory.org/nursing-theorists/Virginia-Henderson  
   Secondary cross-check for Henderson's independence focus and components.

5. Nursing-Theory.org, “Henderson's Nursing Need Theory”: https://nursingtheory.org/theories-and-models/henderson-need-theory  
   Secondary cross-check of the fourteen components and theory concepts.

6. Nursing-Theory.org, “Theories & Models Archive”: https://nursingtheory.org/theories-and-models  
   Broad cross-check of commonly associated theory/model labels; not sole evidence for nuanced claims.

7. Elsevier, *Nursing Theorists and Their Work*, 7th edition book listing: https://shop.elsevier.com/books/nursing-theorists-and-their-work/alligood/978-0-323-05641-0  
   Bibliographic/table-of-contents cross-check. Full book text was not accessed.
