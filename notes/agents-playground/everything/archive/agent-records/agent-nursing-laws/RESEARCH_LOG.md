## Verification findings / caveats
- The source's repeated printed label “Page 1” on images 1 and 2 was retained as observed.
- The “RA 679” entry appears inside the Presidential Decrees section, and is preserved as printed rather than silently corrected.
- “EO 2009” appears for the Family Code in addition to EO 209; this is flagged for verification.
- Some titles/number pairings (including RA 7885 and the RA 8042 amendment note) require further official checking.
- The guide's CPD-unit claims are treated as historical source content, not assumed current.
- The notes do not claim comprehensive legal validation of every listed statute.
