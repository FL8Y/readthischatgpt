## External evidence and how used

### Developmental milestones
- CDC, **Developmental Milestones**, current landing page, https://www.cdc.gov/milestones
- CDC, **Milestones by 9 Months**, https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/9-months.html
- Used to explain milestone ages as developmental guideposts and to distinguish the textbook's exact row wording from current CDC milestone checklists. CDC states milestones describe things most children (75% or more) can do at a given age and are not a substitute for standardized validated screening.

### Newborn reflexes
- American Academy of Pediatrics / HealthyChildren.org, **Newborn Reflexes**, https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/Pages/Newborn-Reflexes.aspx
- Merck Manual Professional Edition, **Physical Examination of the Newborn**, https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/care-of-newborns-and-infants/physical-examination-of-the-newborn
- Used to cross-check reflex elicitation and approximate integration/disappearance ages. AAP's approximate ages differ from the textbook for several reflexes (e.g., rooting, stepping, Moro, palmar grasp, tonic neck). The note preserves textbook values in the source table and flags variability rather than silently replacing them.

### Patient safety
- The Joint Commission, **National Patient Safety Goals**, https://www.jointcommission.org/en-us/standards/national-patient-safety-goals
- The Joint Commission, **National Performance Goals**, https://www.jointcommission.org/en-us/standards/national-performance-goals
- Used to verify that the textbook explicitly describes a 2017 framework and that, for hospitals and critical access hospitals, National Performance Goals replaced the former NPSG chapter effective January 1, 2026. The note keeps historical source content separate from the current-framework update.

### Delegation
- National Council of State Boards of Nursing, **Delegation**, https://ncsbn.org/nursing-regulation/practice/delegation.page
- NCSBN, **National Guidelines for Nursing Delegation**, https://www.ncsbn.org/public-files/NCSBN_Delegation_Guidelines.pdf
- Used to elaborate the textbook's broad responsibilities through the Five Rights of Delegation. This is labeled as an external framework; applicable laws and scope vary by jurisdiction.
