## Synthesis and limitations
- The photographed study guide's documentation-system distinctions broadly align with current open educational explanations of source-oriented documentation, problem-oriented documentation, focus charting, and CBE.
- ADPIE is corroborated by OpenStax and ANA.
- ISMP supports the explicit caution that some abbreviations and symbols are error-prone. The photographed page's OD entry is inherently ambiguous because it lists more than one meaning.
- The image does not reveal the full study-guide title/edition. Berman, Snyder, and Frandsen citations and page numbers are recorded as visible, not independently verified against the cited book.
- The short abbreviation immediately before “(L) Left” is unreadable with sufficient confidence and remains flagged rather than guessed.
- External sources are educational cross-checks, not substitutes for local policy or clinical supervision.
