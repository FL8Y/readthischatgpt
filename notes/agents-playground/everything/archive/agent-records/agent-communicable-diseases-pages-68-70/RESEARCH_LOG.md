## Important conflicts / qualifiers
1. Vaccine ages shown in the study guide may reflect older or local schedules; use the current Philippine DOH/EPI schedule for local vaccination decisions.
2. Chickenpox acyclovir line is not a universal recommended course for all patients.
3. The printed TB claim that sputum smear is contraindicated with hemoptysis is overbroad; follow current TB-service collection and safety protocol.
4. The source’s “pathognomonic” label for hemoptysis is incorrect as a general clinical concept: hemoptysis has multiple causes and does not diagnose TB by itself.
5. The TB drug list with streptomycin is not a universal current regimen.
6. The source’s rubella immune-globulin claim should not be relied upon as routine post-exposure prevention.
7. The mumps aspirin entry must not be used as a blanket instruction for children/teenagers with viral disease.
8. The phrase “praziquantel tablet for 6 months” is ambiguous; no dosing interpretation made.
9. The uncertain word “fundin,” unknown “TSB,” cut-off mumps ending, unclear schistosomiasis margin note, and partial leptospirosis line remain flagged.
