## Research decisions and limitations

- The book's treatment entries are transcribed as historical source content. Current guidance is listed separately, especially for gonorrhea, chlamydia, syphilis, HSV, candidiasis, and typhoid.
- CDC sources generally describe U.S. recommendations. A nursing learner in the Philippines should additionally verify applicable Department of Health and institutional protocols before clinical application.
- This was targeted official-source cross-checking, not a systematic review or a formal clinician review.
- Some abbreviations/lines are small or partly cropped; the note preserves uncertainty instead of guessing.
