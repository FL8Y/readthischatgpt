# Agents' Playground

Welcome, agents. This folder is your designated playground for autonomous exploration and execution.

Repository: https://github.com/FL8Y/readthischatgpt
Canonical project: `notes/`

## Your freedom

You may freely create, edit, reorganize, test, and improve files **inside `notes/agents-playground/`** as needed for your assigned work. You may use subfolders for separate agents, experiments, research, logs, scratch work, datasets, drafts, and verified deliverables.

You may read the rest of the repository to understand the project, its standards, topic hierarchy, dependencies, and connected sources. Reading outside this playground is encouraged when relevant.

## Your boundary

**Your write permission is limited to `notes/agents-playground/` unless the user explicitly grants a wider scope.**

- Do not edit, delete, rename, move, or overwrite files elsewhere in the repository.
- Do not modify another agent's work unless the coordinator explicitly assigns that work to you.
- Give each agent a separate subfolder when multiple agents work concurrently, for example `notes/agents-playground/agent-01/`.
- If a change outside this playground is necessary, record the exact path, proposed change, and reason in your handoff. Do not make the change yourself.
- Never commit secrets, access tokens, credentials, or private information.

## What to do here

1. Read the live repository standards and relevant source-of-truth files before starting.
2. Inventory the full assigned scope and identify relevant repository files, linked sources, connected data, dependencies, gaps, and existing work.
3. Research thoroughly; distinguish verified facts, synthesis, uncertainty, and conflicting sources.
4. Save real deliverables here. Do not substitute a plan, empty template, or chat-only response for actual work.
5. Maintain a detailed checklist and research log so another agent can resume without relying on chat memory.
6. Re-fetch and audit saved files. Mark work DONE only when its required content and verification are complete.
7. Continue through every in-scope item until it is DONE or individually documented as BLOCKED with the reason and recovery attempts.
8. Update your log and leave the exact next action when the session ends.


This is a suggested structure, not a requirement to create empty folders or files. Create only what your work needs. Agents must not write into another agent's subfolder without explicit permission.

## Status rules

- `NOT STARTED`: no substantive work yet.
- `IN PROGRESS`: work exists but has not passed verification.
- `DONE`: deliverable exists and acceptance checks passed.
- `BLOCKED`: a specific limitation prevents completion; document attempted recovery.

Do not claim a file, source, dataset, test, or repository update was verified unless you actually checked it. Do not claim background work continues after your session ends.

**This is your playground: explore deeply, work independently, keep a durable log, verify your results, and respect the boundary.**
