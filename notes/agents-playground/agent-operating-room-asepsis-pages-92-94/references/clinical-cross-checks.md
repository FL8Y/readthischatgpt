## High-impact discrepancy register
1. **121°C for 15 minutes:** source presents it as an “ideal setting.” CDC parameters vary; 121°C/15 minutes must not be used as a universal cycle.
2. **Black stripe proves sterile:** source phrases the answer as “is sterile.” CDC explains that a chemical indicator's change does not alone prove sterility.
3. **Cleaning not required before steam:** source wording is misleading. CDC requires thorough cleaning before sterilization; residue can compromise efficacy.
4. **Circulating nurse has direct contact with sterile field:** the source's “except” answer must not be interpreted as permission for unsterile contact. Role boundaries require maintenance of the sterile field.
5. **All endoscopic instruments simply “sterile”:** classify by tissue contacted/accessory use and follow device-specific directions; a mucosa-contact flexible endoscope commonly requires at least high-level disinfection while accessories penetrating sterile tissue require sterilization.
6. **Sterile-water rinse before disinfectant:** not a universal pre-step for all instruments; use the validated process/device instructions.
